# DingoNet v0.3 review fixes (F1-F6)

Fixes for F1-F6 of `2026-10-27-v0.3-review.md` (the report stays unedited; F7 and F8 are fixed on the LAN discovery
branch). Branch `fix/v0.3-udp-review`, all in `UdpTransport`. Each finding: the change, then the test that pins it
(`tests/UdpTests.cpp`).

| Finding | Status |
|---|---|
| F1 High: any socket error closes the transport | Fixed |
| F2 Medium: half-open connects hold every slot | Fixed |
| F3 Medium: no cap on a peer's outgoing queue | Fixed |
| F4 Medium: read budget ignores empty packets | Fixed |
| F5 Low: `SIO_UDP_CONNRESET` on | Fixed (no test) |
| F6 Low: a failed send warns per packet | Fixed |

## F1: a failed send or receive no longer closes the transport

- Change: when `enet_host_service` returns -1, `Poll` counts the failure and carries on, with at most 4 failed calls
  per `Poll`. `Impl::SocketFailed` warns at most once per 10 s, with the count and the OS error
  (`CurrentNativeSocketError()`, new in `Socket.h`: `WSAGetLastError()` / `errno`). Any call that succeeds clears the
  failing state. Only when every call has failed for 1 s does the transport raise `Error` for every peer
  (`ConnectFailed` for one not yet connected) and `Close`. The records stay alive until `Close` has destroyed the host.
- Tests: `UdpCarriesOnThroughFailedReceives`. `FailIncomingDatagrams(host, 3)` fails three receives through the
  `intercept` hook; all 50 reliable packets still arrive in order, the transport stays open, nobody disconnects, and
  there is one warning. `UdpClosesOnlyWhenTheSocketFailsForASecond`: `BreakSocket` closes ENet's socket. The first
  `Poll` raises nothing; `Disconnected(Error)` arrives no sooner than 1 s later, with one warning and one error, and
  `IsOpen` is then false.

## F2: half-open connects are trimmed, and one address is capped

- Change: after its service loop, `Poll` calls `Impl::TrimPending`. It tracks the ENet peers in a connect that has
  raised no event (`ACKNOWLEDGING_CONNECT` / `CONNECTION_PENDING`, no record), oldest first, keyed by `connectID`.
  It `enet_peer_reset`s the oldest beyond 16, and any older than `ConnectTimeout`. A new
  `UdpParams::MaxConnectionsPerAddress = 32`, clamped to [1, `MaxConnections`], sets ENet's `duplicatePeers`.
- Tests: `UdpResetsHalfOpenConnectsPastSixteen`: 40 clients send one CONNECT and never poll again, against
  `MaxConnections` 20. The host keeps exactly 16 pending, and a real client still connects.
  `UdpResetsAHalfOpenConnectAfterConnectTimeout`: `MaxConnections` 2 and `ConnectTimeout` 0.5. Two half-open clients
  are reset no sooner than about 0.5 s later, then a real client connects.
  `UdpCapsTheConnectionsFromOneAddress`: with a cap of 2 per address, a third client gets `ConnectFailed`.

## F3: a per-peer queue cap

- Change: a new `UdpParams::MaxQueuedBytes = 4 MB`, at least `MaxReliableBytes`. Each packet that `Send` queues gets
  the record as its `userData` and `Impl::Released` as its `freeCallback`, which gives the bytes back to
  `PeerRecord::Queued` when ENet destroys the packet (acked, or the peer reset). `Send` stops queuing past the cap.
  At the next `Poll`, `Impl::DropOverfull` logs an error, raises `Disconnected(Error)`, and calls
  `enet_peer_disconnect_now` and then `Remove`. A record is freed only after its peer is reset (the oversize drop and
  the channel refusal follow the same order), since a queued packet points at it.
- Tests: `UdpDropsAPeerWhoseQueuePassesTheCap`: a 256 KB cap and a client that never polls. Eight 64 KB sends stop at
  320 KB; the next `Poll` drops the peer with `Error` and one error, `QueuedBytes` returns to 0, and the client then
  sees `Disconnected`. `UdpADrainingPeerStaysUnderTheQueueCap`: 300 x 4 KB (1.2 MB) against the same cap, both ends
  polling. Nothing drops, all 300 arrive in order, and `QueuedBytes` ends at 0.

## F4: the read budget charges each event

- Change: each event from `enet_host_service` costs 64 bytes (`EVENT_COST_BYTES`) against `ReadBudgetBytes`, on top
  of the payload a `RECEIVE` adds.
- Test: `UdpReadBudgetCountsEmptyPackets`: `ReadBudgetBytes` 640 and 200 empty reliable packets. At most 10 arrive
  per host `Poll`, and all 200 arrive.

## F5: `SIO_UDP_CONNRESET` off on Windows

- Change: `DisableUdpConnReset(SocketHandle)` is new in `Socket.h`. On Windows it is a `WSAIoctl` with
  `_WSAIOW(IOC_VENDOR, 12)`, since `SIO_UDP_CONNRESET` is not declared in every SDK; on POSIX it does nothing.
  `UdpTransport::Open` calls it on ENet's socket, after `MakeNonInheritable`. An ICMP port unreachable therefore no
  longer fails the next `recvfrom`. Together with F1, a stale address costs nothing but ENet's peer timeout.
- Test: none. Loopback does not reliably produce the ICMP, and F1's tests cover a failing receive.

## F6: one warning per peer, and peers with too few channels are refused

- Change: `PeerRecord::SendFailed` limits a failed `enet_peer_send` to one warning per peer. A CONNECT whose peer has
  fewer channels than the transport's own (`Impl::Channels`) is refused by `Impl::RefuseChannels`, which calls
  `enet_peer_disconnect_now` first. A host logs one warning and raises no event; a client raises `ConnectFailed`. The
  private `Open` takes a `channels` count, and `UdpTestAccess::Open(address, port, listen, channels, error)` makes a
  foreign-like end with fewer channels.
- Tests: `UdpRefusesPeersWithTooFewChannels`: two 1-channel clients against a normal host. Both see `Disconnected`,
  the host raises no event, and there are exactly 2 warnings. `UdpAClientRefusesAHostWithTooFewChannels`: a normal
  client against a 1-channel host gets `ConnectFailed` and never `Connected`. `UdpWarnsOnceForAPeerItCannotSendTo`:
  1-channel ends on both sides. Six unreliable sends that cannot go out give one warning, and a reliable one still
  arrives.

## Test hooks and docs

- `src/Internal/UdpTestAccess.h`: `FailIncomingDatagrams`, `BreakSocket`, `IsOpen`, `PendingPeers`, `QueuedBytes`,
  `Open(..., channels, ...)`. The drop share and the failure count share one `intercept` hook per host.
- `docs/transports.md` (`UdpTransport`): the new caps, the socket-failure rule (it replaces the wrong "never a lost or
  reset datagram"), `SIO_UDP_CONNRESET`, the channel refusal, the once-per-peer send warning, the record lifetime and
  the hooks. `docs/tests.md`: the new coverage.
- The 11 new tests add about 5 s to the Windows Debug run. Loops that only send and poll do not sleep, since a 1 ms
  sleep can take 15 ms on Windows.
